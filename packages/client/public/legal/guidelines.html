Community Guidelines

Updated 22 November 2026 · Community policy v1

Peakcut Community is a place to ask questions, share work and help each other create.

Respect people

Be constructive. No harassment, hate, threats, sexual exploitation, impersonation or sharing private information without permission.

Keep discussions useful

Use the relevant channel, give enough context for questions and avoid repeated promotional posts, spam or scams. Feedback and criticism are welcome when focused on the work.

Respect access and ownership

Do not redistribute subscriber-only material without permission, bypass access restrictions or upload content you do not have the right to use. Never post credentials or payment information.

Personal help and reports

Use a direct conversation with the Peakcut team or email hello@peakcut.ai for personal assistance or a safety report. Do not post customer-specific details in shared channels. Moderators may remove content or restrict access to protect the community.
