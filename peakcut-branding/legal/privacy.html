Privacy Policy

Updated 22 November 2026 · Community policy v1

This policy covers chat.peakcut.ai, the independent Peakcut chat community. Contact the Peakcut team at hello@peakcut.ai for privacy questions or requests. Accounts here are separate from the official Stoat service and other Peakcut products.

Information and visibility

We process your email address, account credentials, username, profile, avatar, messages, uploaded files, memberships and roles. We also process IP addresses, sessions and operational logs to run and protect the community. Public community channels are visible to members who have access. Subscriber and personal-assistance areas use role-based access.

Private conversations

Direct messages are intended for their participants; they are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized administrators with infrastructure access can access stored data when needed for support, safety, security or legal obligations. Do not post passwords, payment details or other sensitive material. Personal assistance is handled in a direct conversation with the Peakcut team, not a shared customer channel.

Hosting and providers

The community application, MongoDB database and uploaded files are hosted on our AWS infrastructure in Frankfurt, Germany. Encrypted backups are held in a private AWS S3 bucket in the same region. Transactional account email is delivered through Amazon SES in the United States. Providers may process data internationally under their applicable terms.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you choose “Allow analytics”. It uses the same web measurement stream as peakcut.ai and may use first-party analytics cookies to recognize visits across these subdomains. Google receives technical usage data, such as general screen categories and device/browser information. We substitute generic page addresses before sending our page events and do not intentionally send message contents, email addresses, private chat identifiers or account-verification/reset tokens. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in this integration. Do Not Track is respected.

You can change this community browser preference below. Disabling it stops analytics on subsequent community page loads; it does not erase previously collected records or change preferences on other websites. You can also clear cookies in your browser. See Google’s privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

We retain active account and community data while needed to provide and protect the service. Routine backups are retained for 14 days; security or legally required records may need longer retention. Deletion from the live system does not immediately remove older backup copies or copies held by other participants. You may request access, correction, deletion or other rights applicable to you by emailing hello@peakcut.ai. We may verify the request before acting.

Browser storage

The client stores sessions, preferences and cached application assets locally so you can stay signed in and use the service. Camera, microphone or notification access requires browser permission when those features are used. This deployment currently does not offer voice or video calls.
